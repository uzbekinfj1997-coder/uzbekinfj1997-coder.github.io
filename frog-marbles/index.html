Frog Marbles

A chain of marbles rolls towards the hole: shoot marbles from the frog and match three of a colour to clear them before they get there. Nine levels, each in its own world (the last around the pyramids of Giza), and Order chain to review Memory Palace lists. Works offline.

Levels

Aim with the mouse and click to shoot. Right-click, the space bar or a click on the frog swaps the marble in its mouth with the one on its back. On a phone: tap where to shoot, tap the frog to swap. F switches full screen on or off.

2048 (game)

A short break: slide the tiles, merge equal numbers and reach 2048. Arrow keys or WASD on a computer, swipe on a touch screen.

0

How to play

  • ←↑→↓ or WASD move all tiles
  • On a touch screen: swipe on the board
  • Two equal tiles that touch merge into one; their sum is added to the score
  • U or Z go back a move; a move taken back doesn't count towards your best
  • F full screen on or off
  • Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score
  • Settings: board size, goal, animation speed, how far you can go back
